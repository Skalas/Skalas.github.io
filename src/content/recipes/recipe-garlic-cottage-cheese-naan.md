## Description
A quick, high-protein garlic flatbread/naan made with only self-rising flour, blended low-fat cottage cheese, salt, and garlic paste. These naans are rolled very thin and expand when pan-cooked.

## Ingredientes (for 24 Naans)
- 550g Self Rising Flour
- 620g Blended Low Fat Cottage Cheese
- 2 Tbsp Salt
- 3 Tbsp Garlic Paste

## Método
1. Blend the low-fat cottage cheese until completely smooth.
2. In a large bowl, mix the blended cottage cheese, self-rising flour, salt, and garlic paste until a cohesive dough forms.
3. Divide the dough into 24 equal portions (using a food scale for accuracy if tracking).
4. Roll each portion out into a VERY thin round shape (approx. 3.5 to 4 inches in diameter). They will expand significantly once they hit the pan.
5. Cook on a hot dry pan (or lightly greased) until bubbles form and both sides are golden brown.

## Notas
- **Grosor:** Deben estirarse muy delgados (VERY thin), ya que se expanden al entrar al sartén.
- **Tamaño:** Se pueden hacer más grandes, pero ten en cuenta que cambiará la información nutricional por unidad.
